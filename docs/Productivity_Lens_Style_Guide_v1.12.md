# PRODUCTIVITY LENS — STYLE GUIDE v1.12

How every Productivity Lens output looks and reads. Content rules (what goes on each page, word budgets, fit check, DTF method) are in the Playbook v1.12, Modules 3B and 4. If this guide and the Playbook disagree on content or page count, **the Playbook wins**.

**What changed in v1.12** (to match Playbook v1.12)
- **Lead with productivity:** headlines, intros, result cards and takeaways follow the Playbook headline hierarchy and story spine. New "never write" items for scale, revenue and compliance headlines.
- The intro always says what happened to value added per employee, including a fall.
- Frontier display relaxed: a company's own value added per employee may be shown on its own page even when it is at or beyond the frontier. Attainment % stays capped at 100.

**Earlier (v1.11)**
- Page 3 is rebuilt around one headline measure, **frontier attainment**, with a gap bar, a "Behind the gap" table and a printed frontier vintage. The five-indicator score, the floor and the fixed FY2022–FY2026 window are gone.
- Every spotlight page 1–4 carries a **page strip**; pages 2–4 carry a **takeaway line**.
- Same thing, same look: the company, the sector median and the frontier keep one colour and one line style on every chart, card and page.
- Extra words and phrases that must never appear (industry frontier, OECD method, "x of y", productivity gain for an Enabler).
- Snapshots are now 10 files (4 page images + 6 cards). Newsletter email section added. BM labels for the page strip and verdicts added.

## 1. VOICE

- **Plain UK English.** Short sentences (aim for under 20 words). One idea per sentence.
- **Lead with productivity.** Every story is about getting more output from people, machines, space and materials. Open with what the company changed and what happened to output or inputs, then say what happened to value added per employee. Scale, revenue and compliance are context, never the story.
- **Inspiring but honest.** Show what worked and what an SME can copy. Never hype; never hide a fall. If results rose but productivity indicators fell, say so plainly.
- **Specific, not generic.** Name the machine, plant, system, place and number. ✗ "invested in automation" ✓ "added a second extrusion line at Taiping".
- **Tie results to an input.** Per worker, per shift, per machine, per unit, per square metre. ✗ "capacity rose to 180,000 pieces a day" alone ✓ "capacity rose to 180,000 pieces a day from the same plant".
- **Active voice.** ✗ "Capacity was increased" ✓ "Farm Fresh increased capacity".
- **Talk to the SME owner.** "you", "your team", "your plant".
- **UK spelling:** organisation, programme, labour, centre, optimise, analyse.
- **One caveat line at most**, in the notes. No hedging inside the story.
- **Say what was reported, nothing more.** A capacity increase is "capacity rose", never "productivity rose". Write that productivity rose only where the report says so. An Enabler (an investment or action with no measured operating change) is described as what the company put in place, never as a productivity gain.

**Never write**
- Rankings or rank words: top, best, leader, leading, ranked, outperform, laggard, worst, No. 1.
- Position language: "x of y", "ranked n", "second in the sector", or any sorted list of companies.
- Investment language: buy, sell, undervalued, share price, target price, recommend.
- Frontier wording: "the industry frontier", "the Malaysian frontier", "the OECD method", "OECD-aligned", "OECD-style". The only approved forms are **"Selected listed-company peer frontier"** and **"inspired by OECD research on frontier firms"**.
- **Headlines, result cards or lessons built on:** revenue, sales or profit growth · store, outlet or customer counts · acquisitions · assets placed in the market · certifications, awards, "zero recalls" or other safety and compliance records · sustainability measures · HR benefits · targets or plans ("expected", "will").
- Jargon without a plain explanation: synergy, leverage (as a verb), holistic, best-in-class, world-class.
- "The company discloses / reports that…" in strengths. Describe the achievement itself.
- A high value added per RM1 staff cost described as a strength when staff cost per employee is below the sector median. Write "lower wage level".
- Exclamation marks, emojis (in the spotlight), ALL CAPS sentences.

## 2. NUMBERS

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| Thousands separator | 180,000 · 1,669 |
| Ringgit | RM233,300 · RM2.70 · RM181.0 million · RM1.1 billion |
| Short forms in tables and cards only | RM47m · 239.5m L (BM: RM47j · 239.5j L) |
| Percentages: one decimal for change | +3.4% · −12.4% (use a real minus sign) |
| Frontier attainment | Whole % only: 63% · capped at 0–100; 100 = "at or beyond the frontier" |
| Value added per employee, frontier, gap | "RM[x] per employee · frontier RM[z] · gap RM[g]" — always the two RM values beside the %; gap RM0 when at or beyond the frontier |
| Points change in attainment | "up from 50% in FY2022" or "+13 points" |
| Ratios | 3.66× · RM2.70 per RM1 |
| Change | "up from 70,000" / "70,000 → 180,000" |
| Years | FY2026 = the financial year covered; give the year-end once on page 1 ("year to 31 March 2026"). DTF years come from the rolling window, never hard-coded. A result from an earlier report always names its year ("FY2024") |
| Frontier vintage | "Frontier vintage: Nov 2026 · 8 companies · 36 company-years · includes this company" |
| Page references | p.23 · pp.39, 45 (BM: hlm.23) — always the **printed** page |
| Missing | "Not found in documents reviewed" (BM: "Tiada dalam dokumen yang disemak") |
| Not calculated | n.c. (explain once in the notes) |

- Never round in a way that changes the story; keep the company's own precision.
- A figure that fell is shown as a fall, never as an achievement.
- Attainment % is capped at 100. A company's **own** value added per employee may be shown as reported on its own page, even when above the frontier value. Never show another company's value on a company's page.
- Verdict wording is fixed (Playbook 3B): **At the frontier · Closing the gap · Holding steady · Gap widening**. Verdict line: verdict + latest attainment + first-year attainment, max 20 words.

## 3. COLOURS

Taken from the current spotlight design. Use these values only. (The website uses the MPC palette pending a decision; these values apply to the spotlight PDF, cards and email.)

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0D1B2A` | Main text, top rule, dark table header, snapshot box background, frontier line and marker |
| Slate | `#2D4258` | Secondary dark panels, sector median line and marker |
| Text grey | `#5E6B7A` | Captions, labels, notes, footers, inactive page-strip items |
| Teal (brand) | `#0F8B7E` | "PRODUCTIVITY LENS" wordmark, section labels, card top borders, Grade A badge, strengths box, company line and markers in charts, current page in the page strip |
| Teal tint | `#E6F3F1` | Strengths box background |

| Coral | `#D9573F` | Areas-to-watch label and border only |
| Coral tint | `#FBE9E5` | Areas-to-watch box background |
| Cream | `#F5F0E6` | Table row stripes, card backgrounds |
| Paper | `#FBF8F2` | Page background |
| Rule | `#E4DDD0` | Table lines, card borders, dividers, gap-bar track |
| Light on dark | `#C4D0DC` | Text on Ink panels |

**Rules**
- Teal = our brand and "good". Coral = "watch" only. Never colour companies as winners or losers.
- **Same thing, same look.** On every chart, card and page: company = Teal, sector median = Slate (dashed in line charts), frontier = Ink (dotted in line charts). Never swap them between pages.
- The DTF verdict is plain Ink text. No traffic-light colours for Closing, Holding or Widening.
- Grade badges: A teal, B amber, C text grey (white letter on the colour). Grade A must be the most prominent badge.
- Charts: no more than 3 lines per chart.
- Contrast: body text always Ink or Text grey on Paper/Cream/white; on Ink panels use white or Light on dark.

| Headline (page 1) | Lora Regular | 22 pt |
| Big numbers (result cards, frontier attainment) | Lora Regular | 27 pt |

| Bold lead-ins | Inter SemiBold / Bold | 10 pt |

| Section labels (KEY RESULTS) and page strip | Inter Bold, caps, letter-spacing 0.08em | 8.5 pt |
| Notes and footers | Inter Regular, Text grey | 8 pt (notes only; never body text) |

- Fallbacks: Lora → Georgia → serif. Inter → Arial → sans-serif.
- Email: system fonts only (Georgia headings, Arial body), body ≥ 14 px, big numbers ≥ 28 px.
- Social cards: text ≥ 28 px.
- **Never shrink type to fit.** Cut words instead (Playbook, fit check).

## 5. PAGE LAYOUT (A4 PDF)

- Size A4 portrait (210 × 297 mm). Margins 16 mm left/right, 14 mm top, 14 mm bottom.
- **Header on every page:** "PRODUCTIVITY LENS" (Teal, bold) | Company | FY, with a 1.5 pt Ink rule below.
- **Footer on every page:** "Page x / y" left; "Source: [Company] Annual Report FY[YYYY]" right; Text grey 8 pt.
- At least **8 mm between blocks**. White space is part of the design; do not fill it.
- Maximum **two columns** of body text. Cards in rows of three on page 1 only.
- Tables: Ink header row with white text; Cream stripes; Rule lines; no vertical lines; numbers right-aligned; page column narrow and right-aligned.
- Boxes: 1 pt Rule border, 3 pt coloured top border (Teal) or left bar (Amber for insights), radius 2 px.
- No company logos, stock photos or photos of people. No clip art.

### Page strip (pages 1–4 only; not on the appendix)

- Sits directly under the header rule, same place and same style on every page, so the reader can follow the story from page to page.
- Text: **1 The story · 2 What they did · 3 Where they stand · 4 What you can do** (BM labels in section 8).
- Current page: Teal, Inter Bold, with a 1.5 pt Teal underline. Other pages: Text grey, Inter Bold.
- Page strip is a label, not a link list: no extra boxes, icons or arrows.

### Takeaway line (pages 2–4)

- One line directly under the page title: the one thing to remember from that page, in productivity terms (output, inputs, value added), never scale or revenue. Max 15 words (page-3 verdict line may run to 20). Plain words, one number where possible, never an "x of y" count.
- Page 1 has no separate takeaway; the headline does that job.
- Takeaways use only figures already in the Content file.

### Page plan (content from Playbook Module 4)

1. **The story** — page strip, label line (SPOTLIGHT · SECTOR · BURSA CODE · YEAR END), headline (headline hierarchy), intro (story spine: the move, then value added per employee), three result cards (at least two operating or productivity results, each with its insight line), productivity snapshot box (Ink panel, 4 indicators with FY, change and sector median or "pending"), one initiative-count line.
2. **Key initiatives** — page strip, title, takeaway line, table of 10 most important Tier 1–2, Grade A–B initiatives, "All [n] initiatives are listed in the appendix" line, two strengths (Teal tint) and two areas to watch (Coral tint) side by side.
3. **Distance to Frontier** (or **Five-year progress**) — see below.
4. **SME lessons** — page strip, title, takeaway line, up to three lesson boxes (only qualifying lessons; never padded), then the notes.
5. **Appendix: all initiatives found** — two-column list grouped by Tier: name · grade badge · page. No paragraphs. No page strip.

**Page 3 order (reads top to bottom: how close, which direction, what sits behind it, what to do next)**

1. Page strip, title, **verdict line** as the takeaway.
2. One big number: latest-year frontier attainment % (Lora 27 pt), with the line "RM[x] per employee · frontier RM[z] · gap RM[g]" beneath it.
3. **Gap bar** (Mode 1 only): one horizontal bar from 0 to the frontier on a Rule-coloured track. Three markers only: company in the first year (Teal ring), company in the latest year (Teal filled), sector median in the latest year (Slate diamond). The frontier end of the bar is marked with an Ink cap. No other company appears. Label markers directly.
4. One line chart over the rolling window (section 6).
5. **"Behind the gap" table:** four diagnostics (revenue per employee, value added per RM1 staff cost, value added per RM1 fixed assets, inventory turnover) with first-year value, latest-year value and sector median. Gap pointers marked with a small Amber bar. Values only, no scores, no colour judgement.
6. Analysis (max 60 words), then the one-line lesson pointer to page 4 where the Playbook allows it.
7. Notes (max 3 lines) with the frontier label, panel size, vintage, "One fixed frontier for the window. Not a ranking. Nominal analyst proxy. Inspired by OECD research on frontier firms; not the OECD method."

**Mode 2 (fewer than 5 companies):** title "Five-year progress"; label "Five-year progress · sector frontier pending"; big number is the value added per employee index (first year = 100); no gap bar, no frontier line, no sector median.

## 6. CHARTS (DTF page and editions)

- One idea per chart. Line chart for the trend; horizontal gap bar for the level.
- Mode 1 line chart: company attainment, sector median attainment and the frontier at 100, across the latest five financial years of the sector panel. Y-axis 0–100. No floor line.
- Frontier line is labelled **"Selected listed-company peer frontier"** (wrap to two lines if needed; never shorten to "industry frontier").
- Label lines directly at the end of the line; no legend box if direct labels fit.
- Light Rule gridlines; no 3D, shadows or gradients.
- Every chart has a source line: "Analyst calculation from company annual reports FY[first]–FY[last]." Years come from the window, never typed by hand.
- Never put a company name on the frontier; never show other companies' names or values on a company's DTF chart.
- Attainment above 100 is never drawn. A year with a scope change is left out of the line.

## 7. SNAPSHOTS, CARDS AND EMAIL

**Ten snapshots per language**

| # | Snapshot | Size |
|---|---|---|
| 01–04 | Pages 1–4 of the spotlight (not the appendix) | A4 at 200 dpi, 1654 × 2339 px |
| 05 | Headline card | 1080 × 1350 px |
| 06 | Productivity snapshot card | 1080 × 1350 px |
| 07 | DTF card | 1080 × 1350 px |
| 08–10 | One SME lesson per card (only qualifying lessons) | 1080 × 1350 px |

**Social cards (1080 × 1350 px)**
- Paper background, 64 px margins, Teal wordmark top left.
- One message per card. Max 3 lines per paragraph, max ~45 words per card.
- Big number in Lora 120–160 px; supporting text Inter ≥ 28 px. The headline card's big number is an operating or productivity result, never revenue, a count of outlets or a compliance record.
- DTF card: attainment % with the two RM values, the verdict line, the trend line, and the frontier vintage in the footer. Same colours as the spotlight chart.
- Bottom line on every card: "Source: [Company] Annual Report FY[YYYY], p.[x]". Every card shows "Productivity Lens".
- Use only figures already confirmed in the self-audit. No logos, stock photos or photos of people.

**Newsletter email**
- Single column, 600 px wide, inline CSS only, no scripts, Style Guide colours.
- System fonts only (Georgia headings, Arial body); body ≥ 14 px; big numbers ≥ 28 px; must read without images.
- The DTF section carries the verdict line and the frontier vintage.
- Footer: "Source: [Company] Annual Report FY[YYYY]. Figures as reported, not independently audited. Not investment advice. DRAFT – not for distribution until reviewed."

## 8. BAHASA MELAYU VERSION

- Standard Bahasa Malaysia (DBP style), formal but friendly; never Bahasa Indonesia.
- Same layout, colours and type sizes as English. **Word budgets are EN − 15%.** Shorten wording; never shrink type.
- Numbers, units, page numbers and company names stay identical. Page references use "hlm.".
- Use the Playbook glossary (Module 4D) for every key term. Brand stays "Productivity Lens".
- Page strip (BM): **1 Kisahnya · 2 Apa yang dilakukan · 3 Kedudukan semasa · 4 Apa yang anda boleh lakukan**.
- Verdicts (BM): Di paras sempadan · Jurang semakin rapat · Jurang kekal stabil · Jurang semakin melebar.
- Frontier label (BM): "Sempadan rakan setara syarikat tersenarai terpilih". Vintage label: "Versi sempadan".
- Check every BM page and card for cut-off text; BM often runs 10–20% longer.
